Based on the Signifi Solutions International Inc. minute book, here's a sequential list of resolutions that can serve as a template for building future corporate minute books:
INCORPORATOR'S WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS


Approval of Articles of Incorporation - Approve the form and content of the Articles of Incorporation


Appointment of Initial Directors - Appoint the first director(s) of the Corporation, effective as of incorporation date


Resignation of Incorporator and Transfer of Corporate Records - Incorporator resigns, delivers corporate records and minute book to initial directors, and authorizes directors to complete organization


FIRST DIRECTORS' RESOLUTIONS (Consent in Writing)


Appointment of Officers - Appoint President/CEO and other officers as determined by the board


Registered Office - Fix the registered office address of the Corporation


Execution of Documents - Authorize signing authority for deeds, transfers, contracts, and other instruments


Share Certificates - Approve and adopt the form of share certificate for common shares


Issuance of Shares to Initial Shareholders - Authorize issuance of common shares to initial shareholders for cash consideration at specified price per share


Banking Resolution - Authorize opening and maintaining bank accounts and execution of banking documents


Financial Year End - Set the financial year end date


Waiver of Public Accountant - Waive appointment of public accountant for current financial year, subject to shareholder approval


Corporate Seal - Adopt corporate seal form (if applicable)


UNANIMOUS WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS OF THE DIRECTORS


Adoption of General By-law No. 1 - Adopt the corporation's general by-law


Appointment of Officers - Formally appoint President and other officers


Corporate Seal - Adopt form of corporate seal (if using)


Registered Office - Confirm registered office address


Issuance of Shares - Issue shares to subscribers per subscription agreements


Subscription Agreements and Share Certificates - Approve forms and authorize execution


Banking Arrangements - Authorize bank account opening and signing authorities


Fiscal Year-End - Fix fiscal year-end date


Public Accountant - Waive appointment of public accountant, subject to shareholder approval


Minute Book - Establish corporate minute book maintenance at registered office


UNANIMOUS WRITTEN RESOLUTIONS OF THE SHAREHOLDERS


Election of Directors - Elect director(s) to hold office until successors are elected


Confirmation of By-law - Confirm General By-law No. 1


Public Accountant - Dispense with appointment of public accountant by unanimous resolution


Number of Directors - Fix the number of directors


Ratification - Approve, ratify and confirm all acts, contracts, proceedings, appointments and elections made by incorporator and Board of Directors


FIRST SHAREHOLDERS' RESOLUTIONS


Election of Directors - Elect director(s) to hold office until successor is elected or appointed


Confirmation of Bylaw - Confirm General Bylaw No. 1 as a bylaw of the Corporation


Waiver of Public Accountant - Dispense with appointment of public accountant by unanimous shareholder resolution


Number of Directors - Fix number of directors until changed in accordance with Articles and Act


This sequential template provides a comprehensive framework for organizing any corporation's initial resolutions, covering all essential corporate governance matters from incorporation through initial operations setup.
